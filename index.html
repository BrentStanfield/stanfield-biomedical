About Us

Stanfield Biomedical LLC provides contract preclinical research services with deep expertise in virology, immunology, and oncology. Our team combines academic rigor with industry-focused execution to deliver high-quality efficacy data that supports your development program.

Based in Louisiana, we leverage specialized expertise in guinea pig models — offering capabilities that most mouse-focused CROs cannot provide. Our viral oncology background gives us unique insight into virus-associated cancers and infectious disease applications.
